I reconstruct past changes in Arctic vegetation, carbon cycling, and hydrology using organic geochemical proxies, stable isotopes, radiocarbon dating, and Bayesian modeling. I also study geochemical proxy systems in modern Arctic settings to imrpove our understanding of how these tools record a range of environmental conditions, both in the present and past.
